How to Use SmartReply

Step by Step
  1. 1
    Open Reply in Gmail

    Open an email and click Reply or Reply All.

  2. 2
    Click the SmartReply icon

    Find the SmartReply icon in your compose toolbar right next to Gmail's Send button.

  3. 3
    Select contextual reply options

    SmartReply generates multiple response choices. Select any response to preview its complete draft.

  4. 4
    Insert & review yourself

    Click Insert draft to place the text into Gmail. You always review before sending.

Pro Tips

💡 Custom Instructions: Type notes like "Agree to 2pm on Thursday" to guide the draft.

🔒 Privacy: Thread content is sent to Gemini only when you open the draft panel by clicking the icon.
