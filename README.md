# SmartReply AI — Professional Gmail Reply Assistant (Chrome Extension)

An executive, lightweight, and modern Chrome Extension (Manifest V3) that injects a **1-click AI reply assistant** directly into your Gmail formatting & action toolbars (right next to formatting options, Loom, and attachment buttons).

---

## ⚡ Features

- **💼 Executive & Clean UI:** Styled in deep slate & royal blue, designed to match professional email workflows.
- **📎 Toolbar Integration:** Embeds right alongside native Gmail attachment icons, formatting tools, and third-party extensions like Loom.
- **⚡ 1-Click Intent Chips:**
  - ⚡ **Auto Smart Reply:** Intelligently reads incoming email thread context to craft a tailored, natural response.
  - ✓ **Confirm & Accept:** Confirm receipt and agree to proceed with clear next steps.
  - ✕ **Polite Decline:** Courteous, articulate decline or request to reschedule.
  - 💬 **Acknowledge & Thanks:** Professional acknowledgment and gratitude.
  - 📅 **Schedule Meeting:** Propose times and offer availability to connect.
  - ✍️ **Custom Directive:** Type a brief 2-word instruction (e.g., *"Say Thursday 2 PM works and ask for the deck"*) and draft instantly.
- **🔒 Direct & Private:** Connects straight from your browser to Google Gemini API using your free API key.

---

## 🚀 Setup & Reload Instructions

### 1. Reload the Extension in Chrome
1. In Google Chrome, navigate to `chrome://extensions/`.
2. Find **SmartReply AI — Email Assistant for Gmail** and click the **🔄 Reload** button (or click **Load unpacked** and select `C:\Users\PC 3\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\gmail-reply-extension`).

### 2. Enter and Verify API Key
1. Click the **SmartReply AI** icon in Chrome's toolbar.
2. Paste your Gemini API key from [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey).
3. Click **Verify Key** — you will immediately see `✓ Key is valid!`.
4. Click **Save Settings**.

### 3. Using in Gmail
1. Refresh [Gmail](https://mail.google.com/).
2. Open any email thread and click **Reply**.
3. In the bottom formatting toolbar (where attachments and Loom are located), click **AI Reply**.
4. Choose an intent chip or enter custom instructions to generate and insert the reply instantly!
